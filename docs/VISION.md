# Vision

Diseino aims to become a sustainable independent software studio: small by design, technically capable, and focused on software that solves real problems.

Over time, the ambition is to:

- Develop multiple useful products that can sustain their continued development.
- Make capable software accessible and affordable to individuals, small teams, and communities.
- Integrate AI where it improves research, development, automation, or the software itself.
- Share useful tools, components, and technical knowledge with developers.
- Support individuals and communities through practical technology.

Open-source participation is one part of this ambition. Diseino also develops proprietary products; commercial sustainability and community contribution are intended to coexist. The portfolio and contribution model will develop through experience rather than assumed scale.
