# Governance

DiseinoCommunity is currently founder-led and independently maintained by the founder of Diseino Technologies. The founder makes final decisions about direction, publication, project quality, and maintenance.

People are welcome to raise issues, suggest changes, contribute, and join project discussions. Participation does not imply shared ownership or decision authority across the initiative.

As projects and their communities grow, a project may adopt a different governance or maintainer model, documented by that project. Until then, the founder is the final decision-maker, and maintenance capacity is limited by the solo-founder structure.
