# Community

DiseinoCommunity is at its establishment stage. Participation will grow alongside the projects, and channels may differ by repository.

As projects are published, people can participate by:

- Using software and sharing practical feedback.
- Reporting bugs and suggesting improvements.
- Improving guides, examples, and documentation.
- Submitting focused code changes and pull requests.
- Sharing ideas and discussing possible directions.
- Collaborating where scope and maintenance capacity allow.

Start with the relevant project's README, contribution guidance, and issue or discussion channels. Not every idea will become a project, and submissions may need revision or may not be accepted. See [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md) and the [Code of Conduct](../CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
