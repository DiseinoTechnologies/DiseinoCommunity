# Projects

The DiseinoCommunity portfolio is yet to be developed. This page describes possible work; it does not announce projects that do not exist.

Possible areas include:

- **Developer tools and utilities** for common tasks.
- **AI tools, experiments, and integrations** exploring practical uses of AI.
- **Automation and productivity utilities** for individuals and small teams.
- **Reusable libraries and components** solving focused problems.
- **Educational resources and technical documentation** sharing useful knowledge.
- **Community-driven software** shaped with its users and contributors.

Diseino also intends to contribute to existing open-source projects when it can make useful, well-scoped contributions.

## Separate from proprietary products

Diseino's proprietary product initiatives are not DiseinoCommunity projects unless specific components are deliberately published under an identified licence. CineSense and DiseinoPresenter are current working names for proprietary initiatives. Their code, internal architecture, and private documentation are outside this repository and its MIT License.

See [Open Source](OPEN_SOURCE.md) for project principles.
