# Code of Conduct

DiseinoCommunity aims to be a respectful, constructive place to build and share useful software. Participation is open regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and work without attacking people.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful.
- Respect maintainers' responsibility for project scope and decisions.
- Respect privacy; do not publish private information without consent.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, insults, persistent disruption, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Raising a concern

Report conduct concerns privately to repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not post sensitive details publicly. The founder currently leads and maintains DiseinoCommunity and will review reports, taking action needed to protect the community.

This code applies to DiseinoCommunity spaces and public spaces when someone represents the initiative.
