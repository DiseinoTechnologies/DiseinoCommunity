# DiseinoCommunity

**Useful, capable software should be accessible to more than organizations with large budgets.**

DiseinoCommunity is the public community and open-source initiative of Diseino Technologies, an independent software studio at an early stage of development. It is a place to publish useful software, share technical knowledge, and contribute to the wider open-source ecosystem.

The initiative is being established. Its public project portfolio is not yet developed; this repository sets out the principles and expectations that will guide it.

## What belongs here

Over time, DiseinoCommunity may include developer tools and utilities, AI experiments and integrations, automation, reusable libraries, educational resources, and contributions to existing projects. These are areas of intent, not claims about work already available. See [Projects](docs/PROJECTS.md) and the [Roadmap](docs/ROADMAP.md).

## About Diseino

Diseino Technologies is an independent, solo-founder software studio building toward a portfolio of useful software products. It aims to combine sustainable product development with meaningful contributions others can use and improve.

Diseino follows an AI-native development approach: AI is intended to be practical in research, engineering, and product work, with people responsible for direction, review, and decisions. See [About Diseino](docs/ABOUT.md) and the [Vision](docs/VISION.md).

## Proprietary products and community projects

CineSense and DiseinoPresenter are proprietary product development initiatives, separate from DiseinoCommunity. Their source code, internal architecture, private documentation, and other non-public materials are not covered by this repository or its licence.

Only work intentionally published through DiseinoCommunity is a community project. Each project will state its scope, status, licence, and maintenance expectations. Product names are development names and may change. See [Open Source](docs/OPEN_SOURCE.md).

## Who this is for

DiseinoCommunity aims to serve developers, creators, individuals, small organizations, and communities seeking practical software and knowledge. Participation will grow alongside the projects; see [Community](docs/COMMUNITY.md) and [Governance](docs/GOVERNANCE.md).
